A Journey Through Time

2007 – 2013

The Ground Floor

Musnad began his career in 2007 as a call center agent — no shortcuts, just the phones. Over the next six years he moved through HMC Technologies, Legato Services Ltd, Digicon Technologies, Symphony Mobile (Edison Group), and Rocket Internet GmbH, rising from agent to operations supervisor, then quality analyst, and eventually Head of Customer Service. Every rung of that ladder shaped how he thinks about customer experience today.

2013 – 2022

Founding SkyTech

In September 2013, Musnad founded SkyTech Solutions from Uttara, Dhaka, on a simple belief: outsourcing shouldn't only benefit Fortune 500 companies. Nearly a decade later, SkyTech had grown into a 500+ person operation delivering voice, chat, email, and back-office support to clients across the US, Australia, and Canada.

2022 – Present

Industry Leadership

Elected Director of the Bangladesh Association of Contact Center and Outsourcing (BACCO) in 2022 and elevated to Joint Secretary-General in 2024, Musnad now helps shape national policy for the country's outsourcing sector — while growing the SkyTech umbrella into Marketorr, Codezzi, SkyTex, and Agency Handy.
